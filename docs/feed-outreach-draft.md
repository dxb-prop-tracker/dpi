**Subject: Licensed listing-data feed — Dubai residential analytics platform**

Dear [name],

We are building a Dubai residential analytics platform for investors and buyers, built on the full Dubai Land Department open-data register — every recorded sale, every registered project and its completion history, Ejari rents and Mollak service charges. The register tells us what property actually trades for; what it cannot tell us is what sellers are currently asking, and how those asks are moving.

We would like to license a listing-data feed from you to close that gap. Specifically:

- a daily (or weekly) snapshot of live Dubai sale listings — asking price, area, project/building, bedrooms, size, Trakheesi permit number, and the listing URL;
- ideally with 6–12 months of price history as a backfill;
- rent listings as a possible second phase.

Two points that may matter to you commercially. First, we do not rehost listings or store agent contact details — every listing on our platform links out to yours, so the lead and the relationship stay with your agents. Second, our platform pairs each asking price with the recorded sales history of the same building from the land registry, which makes your listings more credible to serious buyers, not less.

Could we set up a short call to discuss feed formats, coverage and pricing? A one-page technical spec of what we ingest is attached.

Kind regards,
[signature]
